Welcome to Rashtriya Clinical Laboratory Prabandhan

Our laboratory brings together modern diagnostic equipment with a patient-first approach. Every sample — whether collected at home or at our centre — passes through the same rigorous quality checks before a report is generated, so you can trust the numbers whether it's a routine blood test or a full body checkup

We understand that visiting a lab can be stressful, especially when you're unwell or short on time. That's why we've built our services around convenience: online registration that takes minutes, flexible sample collection options, and digital reports you can access as soon as they're ready — no waiting in line required.

Whether you need a single test, a doctor-prescribed panel, or a comprehensive annual health checkup, our team of experienced pathologists and technicians is here to guide you through the process and answer any questions along the way.
