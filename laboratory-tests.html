Some Important Terms

CBC

This blood test measures your red blood cells, white blood cells, and platelets. It helps doctors check your overall health and detect conditions like anemia, infections, and bleeding disorders


HbA1c

This test measures your average blood sugar levels over the past 2 to 3 months. It is primarily used by doctors to diagnose prediabetes and monitor diabetes control.


LFT

This panel measures specific enzymes, proteins, and bilirubin in your blood. It evaluates how well your liver is working and identifies issues like jaundice or fatty liver


KFT

Also called a renal function test, it checks levels of waste products like creatinine and blood urea. It determines how effectively your kidneys are filtering and cleaning your blood


ECG

This quick, non-invasive test records the electrical signals of your heart. It maps your heart's rhythm to help doctors detect heart attacks, irregular heartbeats, and poor blood flow
